OVERVIEW

WHERE DOMINO'S WEB ORDERING SLOWS DOWN

Food ordering fails quietly, in small delays and repeated steps. Me and my team redesigned Domino's web flow to remove those points of friction by leading with the menu, surfacing customization costs early, and collapsing payment into a faster, clearer checkout.

INSIGHTS

USING JAKOB NIELSEN'S USABILITY HEURISTICS

We applied Nielsen's heuristics to evaluate each stage of the selected flows, identifying where the interface violated core usability principles, such as unclear feedback, hidden controls, and unnecessary friction. This structured approach helped prioritize fixes that would have the most impact on user experience.

Through heuristic evaluation and task analysis, three patterns consistently surfaced across ordering, customization, coupons, and payment flows.

🔁 Repeated inputs

Location and address are requested multiple times, creating unnecessary friction.

⛳️ Key actions hidden

Customization costs, coupon effects, and payment status are hidden or unclear.

🐌 Context switching disrupts momentum

Redirects during coupons and payment fragment the experience.
